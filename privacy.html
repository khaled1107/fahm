Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Fahm is a Quran comprehension app built to help Muslims understand the message of the Quran. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive the following from your Google account:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile picture (if available)

We also store the following data you generate while using Fahm:

  • Which surahs you have marked as complete
  • Your reading progress and last read timestamp
  • Your language and display preferences

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect payment information
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not collect any sensitive personal information beyond what is listed above

How we use your information

Your information is used solely to provide the Fahm service:

  • To identify your account and save your reading progress across devices
  • To remember your language and display preferences
  • To restore your progress when you return to Fahm

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the app experience.

Data storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase, a managed database platform. Data is stored on servers in the United States and European Union. Supabase is SOC 2 compliant and follows industry-standard security practices.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties. Your data is never used for advertising purposes.

Anonymous use

You can use Fahm without signing in. In that case, your reading progress is saved locally on your device only and is not associated with any account.

Deleting your data

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us at khaled@tryfahm.com. We will process your request within 30 days.

Children's privacy

Fahm is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Fahm after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at khaled@tryfahm.com.
