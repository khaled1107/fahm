Bismillāh

The Quran is the speech of Allah ﷻ, Al-'Alim — the All-Knowing — and Al-Hakim — the All-Wise. No translation can fully capture it. No commentary can exhaust it.

Fahm is a humble attempt to make its message accessible to those of us who grew up reciting it without fully understanding what we were saying. It is not a scholarly authority — there will be gaps and mistakes. We seek Allah's ﷻ forgiveness for any error, and ask Him to keep us close to the truth.
