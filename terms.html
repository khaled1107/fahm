About Fahm

Fahm is a Quran comprehension tool designed to help Muslims understand the message of the Quran. It provides plain-language explanations, historical context, and verse translations drawn from authenticated scholarly sources.

Fahm is an educational aid, not a religious authority. For matters of Islamic law, rulings, or scholarly questions, please consult a qualified Islamic scholar.
